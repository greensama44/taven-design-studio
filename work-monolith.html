Monolith needed one idea strong enough to hold a whole company together. We found it in a single cube, and built everything else from it.

01

The challenge

A growing company with five products, five looks and no shared voice. Every new launch started from scratch, and nobody outside the team could tell the products belonged together.

02

The idea

One modular shape. The cube becomes the logo, the grid, the icon set and the motion language, so every product can look like itself and still unmistakably like Monolith.

The result

A single identity system the in-house team now uses for every launch, with guidelines short enough that people actually read them.
